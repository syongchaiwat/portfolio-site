Glassy steel blue and cyan with lilac reflections and a coral flare: creased, sharp and high energy.

- **Reference:** the blue and orange glass image.
- **Use when:** you want the most memorable first screen, and accept that the background competes more with the content.
- **Character:** the only option that leaves the purple family. `accent` moves to cyan and `accent-light` to coral.
- **Drift:** the preset's `speed` is .05. Creases slide across each other, so motion is more visible than the number suggests.
- **Consumer provides:** a positioned container and `data-theme="prism"` on it or an ancestor.
- **Do:** use the flat `scrim` everywhere below the hero.
- **Don't:** add coral to buttons or large fills. It works because it is rare.
