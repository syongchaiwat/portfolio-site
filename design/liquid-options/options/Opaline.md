Iridescent droplets floating over a periwinkle to aqua field: the only light option, and a different shader from the other three.

- **Reference:** the pastel droplet image.
- **Use when:** you want light mode, or a friendlier first impression. Pairs with Molten behind the site's existing theme toggle.
- **Character:** airy and playful. Dark text on a pale `scrim`.
- **Drift:** fourteen droplets merge and split on slow independent paths. The preset's `speed` is .07.
- **Consumer provides:** a positioned container and `data-theme="opaline"` on it or an ancestor.
- **Do:** keep content on `scrim` or `surface`. The magenta rims are saturated enough to hurt small text directly on them.
- **Don't:** use it as the only theme if the site should read as senior and technical first. It is the most decorative option.
