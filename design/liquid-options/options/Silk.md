Wide cobalt satin with soft pink light: long horizontal folds with no hard creases.

- **Reference:** the blue and pink flowing image.
- **Use when:** you want the calmest, most premium feel, and a move away from pure violet.
- **Character:** mid-bright, so it needs the strongest dark `scrim` of the fold options relative to how much of the image stays visible.
- **Drift:** the preset's `speed` is .045, the slowest of the four. The folds are stretched 2.4 times along one axis, which reads as fabric.
- **Consumer provides:** a positioned container and `data-theme="silk"` on it or an ancestor.
- **Do:** use `accent-light` pink only for eyebrows and gradient text. Blue carries the interface.
- **Don't:** raise the speed above 1.5. Large folds moving quickly start to feel like water sloshing.
