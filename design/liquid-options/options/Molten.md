Deep violet molten metal with thin bright crests: the closest option to the site's current `#7c3aed` to `#a855f7` identity.

- **Reference:** the dark violet chrome image.
- **Use when:** you want the site to feel like itself, only richer. Recommended default for dark mode.
- **Character:** mostly dark, so text is easy to keep readable. Highlights are thin and rare.
- **Drift:** the preset's `speed` is .055. Slow enough that you notice the change only after a few seconds of watching.
- **Consumer provides:** a positioned container and `data-theme="molten"` on it or an ancestor.
- **Do:** keep `accent` as `#a855f7` with `on-accent` text. White text on that fill only reaches 4.0:1.
- **Don't:** drop the `scrim`. Crests get bright enough to cut through small text.
